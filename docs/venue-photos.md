# Venue photographs — what each one shows

Michele's photographs of Kinepolis Antwerp during Devoxx, sent to the agent in chat (28 Sep 2026).
The images themselves were not committed (they are the photographers' work); this is what the build
takes from each, so the reference survives the session.

## Exterior — "probably useless, but save it"

- Pale grey panel facade; a long curtain wall of glass in **white frames** at ground level, taller
  glazed bays above.
- **KINEPOLIS EVENTCENTER** in large blue channel letters over the entrance, a big white star on the
  roofline above it.
- Two vertical white **KINEPOLIS** banners on poles in front of the facade; a Devoxx beach flag by
  the doors.
- A row of dark grey **bollards** along the pavement edge (the sim's `FORECOURT_BOLLARDS`), brick
  pavers, a concrete stair at the right-hand end of the building.
- Grey sky, attendees with backpacks walking in.

Use: the view out through the entrance doors (the backdrop), if anyone looks back out.

## Reception (two photos) — "the reception, finally!"

- A **monolithic white counter**, solid to the floor, square-edged, with a warm **LED strip under
  its top lip**; a return running towards the doors.
- Behind it a **vertical timber-slat wall** with **two wall screens**: "Pickup your Devoxx polo
  during lunch · Exhibition hall" over a photo of two people.
- The soffit over the desk glows **warm orange**; white box beams and thick white square columns.
- **White table lamps** (drum shades) on the counter end.
- Over the entrance lane, a **large white ring pendant** — a drum with its light underneath.
- **Black ceiling**, dark grey carpet, **black retractable-belt stanchions** marking the lane from
  the doors; a **dark navy wall** on the right with a screen and a small sign.
- The glass doors at the end of the lane, trees in autumn red outside.
- Staff in dark red Devoxx polos behind the counter.

## Room 8 — the keynote cinema

- A **steeply raked auditorium**: rows climb from the stage to the back; the aisles are **stairs**
  with step lights, and a balcony rail part-way up.
- The stage is a **wide floor at the bottom**, dark, with big 3D **#DEVOXX** letters standing on it,
  a lectern left of centre and the speaker in front.
- A **huge slightly curved screen** above the stage, almost the room's width: the talk on one half,
  the slide on the other, a sponsor strip along the bottom (and the DEVOXX logo at its right).
- **Black walls**, a **black ceiling** with a grid of small round downlights; a lighting **truss
  tower** on the left with fixtures on it; wall speakers.
- Some rows have little **tables** between seats (laptops).

## What this changes in the build

These are corrections to guesses, not mood notes. They are cheap to honour and they are exactly
what the "sense of place" critic will look for.

1. **Zaal signage is a large orange panel with one big white numeral**, set beside the
   auditorium entrance and reading as a colour block from down the corridor — not a small sign.
   Secondary wayfinding is a separate **blue** sign with white text and arrows, in Dutch
   (`uitgang zaal 6/7`, `info`). Build both. The orange numeral panels are what make the corridor
   read as Kinepolis from the diorama camera.
2. **The corridor is dark navy carpet and charcoal walls**, not the prototype's red-brown. Its
   ceiling is pale curved plaster vaults springing from square dark columns along both sides, and
   the main staircase sits under a **white tensile fabric canopy** uplit blue. Orange backlit
   poster boxes are the only warm accents.
3. **The main staircase is blue-carpeted and split into three runs** by slim tubular steel
   handrails, with a lighter nosing stripe on each tread.
4. **The exhibition hall is a pale square-column grid** under a white coffered ceiling with
   recessed square lights and a raised illuminated tray soffit; floor is mid-gray carpet, not
   concrete. Dark and empty (ch 2) it goes near-black with red accent panels on the side walls and
   track spots on the beams.
5. **Reception is warm**: wood-slat back wall, long white counter, cream-shaded table lamps, a
   large white pendant disc, an orange ceiling soffit strip.
6. **The catering counter has a blue LED rope along its front and rows of yellow-orange soup cups
   on top**, with big pale lowercase wall graphics behind it. That is the tomato-soup beat, already
   in the venue.

Everything here is reference for materials, colour and dressing. **Room positions, proportions and
both staircase positions still come from `plans/*.png` via `src/sim/geometry.ts`** — these photos
never override the plans.
